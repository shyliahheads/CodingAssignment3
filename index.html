Homemade Hamburgers

Recipe by: Shyliah Heads, adapted from a classic homemade hamburger recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 4 hamburger buns
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 4 slices of cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato slices
  • Pickles
  • Ketchup
  • Mustard

Equipment

  • Large mixing bowl
  • Skillet or grill
  • Spatula
  • Plate
  • Measuring spoons

Directions

  1. Place the ground beef in a large mixing bowl.
  2. Add the salt and black pepper and gently mix until combined.
  3. Divide the mixture into four equal portions and shape each portion into a patty.
  4. Cook the patties on a skillet or grill until they are fully cooked.
  5. Place a slice of cheese on each patty during the final minute of cooking.
  6. Toast the hamburger buns if desired.
  7. Place each cooked patty on a bun and add lettuce, tomato, pickles, ketchup, and mustard.
  8. Serve the hamburgers while warm.
