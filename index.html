Homemade Hamburgers: A Classic Favorite

By Shyliah Heads

Homemade hamburgers are a classic favorite that can be prepared with simple ingredients and customized with your favorite toppings.

Recipe inspiration:

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Hamburger buns
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cheese (optional)
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Your favorite condiments

Directions

  1. Prepare the ingredients and toppings.
  2. Season the ground beef and shape it into patties.
  3. Cook the patties thoroughly in a pan or on a grill.
  4. Add cheese if desired and prepare the hamburger buns.
  5. Assemble the hamburgers with your favorite toppings.
  6. Serve and enjoy.
